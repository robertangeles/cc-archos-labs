After the two WordPress batches ([[2026-10-24-wordpress-named-stories-to-hypotheticals]]), the owner asked to scrub the remaining generic "I've seen this pattern" lines and the "Real Example" / "Case Study" sections about unnamed companies. A first-person regex could not see the second kind, so the sweep read whole posts instead. It covered **every live post**, not just WordPress ones.

## What was done

- 100 live posts had a candidate paragraph (regex for first-person experience, for unnamed-company anecdotes with figures, and for "Real Example" style headings). Five agents each read 20 full posts and wrote edits; a validator then checked every edit against the live text (substring present exactly once, paragraph exists) before any SQL was built.
- **231 paragraph edits across 91 posts**, applied 2026-10-24 UTC in one guarded transaction that commits only if every post still matches the text that was read (md5 of `content_md`). Backup first: `~/.gstack/backups/archos_pdb_post_2026-10-25_pre-sweep.dump`. Nothing archived or deleted.
- First-person claims ("I've watched…", "my clients", "we rolled out…") became generic statements or explicit hypotheticals. Unnamed-company anecdotes became "Consider / Picture / Imagine a…" with the numbers kept as illustration and the tense matched to the surrounding paragraphs. Headings that vouched for them ("A Real Example:", "Case Study:", "Real-World…") became "Example", "Scenario" or "An Example".
- Left alone on purpose: opinions and recommendations in first person, and named organisations, people and studies with a public source (Capital One breach, MIT, Oreg 2003, a quoted TechCrunch interview). **Named claims were not verified.**
- Verified afterwards on PROD: 91 rows touched, 332 posts live, and the only remaining phrase match is a quoted hypothetical in `ai-decision-making-is-the-new-corporate-alibi`.

## Needs a human decision (not changed)

- `consistent-metric-definitions-strategy`: the argument rests on figures tied to named companies and people that look invented (UnitedHealth $120M, Salesforce 18%, Adobe 22%, "Gartner 47 definitions", "TDWI 2026 7.3%", "Former IBM Data VP Sarah Chen"). Verify or cut.
- `ai-technical-debt-blocks-product-launch`: almost entirely named claims that look unverifiable (a Shopify "2025 post-mortem" at 62%, "Sarah Chen" and "Maria Rodriguez", an Anthropic "2024 technical blog", Gartner "AI Debt: The Innovation Tax", a "2025 State of ML Engineering" report). Only its unnamed anecdotes were edited. Verify or cut.
- `copilot-access-controls`: the body is duplicated (paragraphs 29-56 repeat 2-28). Both copies were edited identically; one should be deleted.
- Posts that are now mostly illustration with little else: `stop-collecting-start-connecting`, `build-for-decisions-not-reports-why-legacy-bi-fails-front-lines`, `why-ai-projects-fail-before-they-start-c449`, `ai-doesnt-scale-until-your-org-does-why-teams-fail-models`, `dont-automate-the-soul-out-of-your-business`, `governance-is-culture-not-compliance-why-leadership-matters`.
- `the-roi-of-psychological-safety`: "The ROI isn't hypothetical. It's mathematical." and the 3x / 47% / 64% / 40% bullets now contradict the hypothetical stories above them.
- `ai-success-requires-executive-ownership-not-just-approval`: "approximately 70% of cases" is an unsourced statistic attached to a vague claim of observation.
- `bad-data-is-a-symptom-not-a-root-cause-of-broken-systems` and `transformation-fails-when-you-dont-translate`: many "Consider / Picture" openers in a row may read as repetitive.
- Other unverified named figures left in place: McKinsey 15-20% in `data-quality-metrics`, and IBM, Cloudera, Bowmark and BCG's Fergus Jarvis in `proprietary-data-advantage-matters-more-than-model-choice`.
- The "Metis" byline on all live posts is still an open decision tied to the pipeline redesign.

## Limits

The sweep is phrase-based plus an agent read of every flagged post. A story that matched neither pattern could remain, and an agent could misjudge a borderline case; the before/after for every edit was kept in the session scratchpad, not the repo. Restore any post's `content_md` from the backup above to undo it.
